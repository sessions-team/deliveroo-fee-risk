**Source:** Email from Liam Gillanders (Deliveroo) → Ellie Wilson, Tue 7 Jul 2026, subj "Sessions Q3 Commission".
**Applies to:** sites that launched *after* the start of Q3 2026 (i.e. no preceding-quarter performance data, so a standard Ops adjustment is applied). Existing-estate sites are on their individually-banded rates in the Q3 Commission Output (`03_source-data/Q3_existing-sites_commission_output_2026-07-01.csv`).

## Reconciliation vs contract

Every bracket = **Contract Headline − 1.50%**, where the 1.50% = JBP/Marketer Ads reduction (−0.50%) + a standard Ops adjustment (−1.00%).

✅ **All 9 brackets reconcile exactly** to the contract Headline Commission Rate table (the banded rates that apply when the Price Competitiveness Condition is met). The −1.00% Ops adjustment for new sites sits well within the contract's allowed adjustment ranges (RWT −2.00%→+0.80%, Missing Items −2.40%→+1.60%, Open Hours −1.20%→0.00%).

**Note:** the brackets here are stated as £12–14, £14–16 etc. (rounded), vs the contract's £12–13.99, £14–15.99. Same bands, just notation.

**Q3 vs Q2:** the new-site rate card is **identical to Q2 2026** — same nine final rates, same −1.50% total adjustment. New sites get the standard −1.00% Ops + −0.50% JBP regardless of quarter (they have no preceding-quarter performance to band on).
